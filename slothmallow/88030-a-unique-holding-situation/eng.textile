A Unique Holding Situation

Author: slothmallow

*000*2023-04-13 05:02

So, I found myself in quite the unique situation today, and I figured everyone on here would love to here about it. ❤️

I recently started seeing an endocrinologist, and they ordered a 24 hour urine test for me. They sent me home with a jug, a "toilet hat" and instructions to collect all of my urine for 24 hours.

For those unfamiliar with a "toilet hat" it's like a little bowl that you put on the toilet seat that catches urine. It also has measurements on it. You can get them super cheap on Amazon. People usually use them when they need to catch a kidney stone or something so that the stone can be tested. (Stones made of different things indicate different health issues.) I'm using mine, not for stones, but in order to get every single drop of pee that comes out of me in a 24 hour period and pour it into a jug.

It's odd knowing exactly how much I'm peeing in a day. Today, I've drank about 56 oz, and I've peed about 32 oz. My pee is pretty yellow, which makes sense, since it was nearly 80 degrees today, and I spent most of the day walking around outside.

Which brings me to the unique holding situation I found myself in today. I had originally intended to stay at home all day for the urine test. Once you put pee in the jug, it has to stay refrigerated at all times, and even if it didn't, I don't exactly want to carry around a jug of pee all day. Bringing it to the doctor's office on the train tomorrow will be awkward enough. So, I had decided not to leave home at all...

But then life happened. Errands came up that I had to run today. I had to drop something off at another doctor's office. I had to print something at the library. I had to mail something at the post office. I had to stop by the grocery store. I had to pick up a prescription at the pharmacy. I had to pick up a package. A ton of things that needed to be done today just popped up. The issue was, once I left home, I couldn't use the bathroom at all. I couldn't "waste" any of the urine that needed to go in the jug. So, I was just going to have to hold it.

I was expecting that I would get rather desperate during the day, because I was out and about for around 8 hours, but I actually didn't. Again, I attribute this to the day being rather hot, and me being physically active walking and standing for most of the time. By the time I got home to my "toilet hat" and my jug, my need was, at most, a 4. I also limited my fluid intake, because I really couldn't risk ruining the test by getting myself desperate enough that pain drove me to use the toilet while I was out. I'm now drinking a ton to make up for being dehydrated for most of the day and to be able to actually provide the doctor with enough urine for the test.

So, yeah, that's the story. I wanted to share it, because it's literally a perfect desperation scenario. Like, it almost seems fake/too good to be true, and I got a kick out of this weird situation that I got myself into. I knew people on this site would get a kick out of it, too, and I also figured it could make a good fic prompt for someone. If anyone decides to use this as a fic prompt or something, please do tag me. 😊
