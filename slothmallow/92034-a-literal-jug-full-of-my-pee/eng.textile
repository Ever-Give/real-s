A few months ago, I made a post about a 24 hour urine test that I had to do. It required me to collect the contents of my bladder in a jug that my doctor gave me for 24 hours. I thought that everyone on here might like to see what the jug looked like at the end of the 24 hours when it was completely full of my pee.

One fun detail is that the jug had measurements on the outside, so you can see exactly how much pee came out of me in 24 hours. Along with the pics, I took a couple of videos so you can see the "sloshing". Even though I had to keep the jug refrigerated for the 24 hours there was still quite a pee smell when it was open, so if that's something you're into, you can imagine that. 😉

I also included pics of the "toilet hat" that I had to put over the toilet seat in order to catch the pee and then pour it into the jug.

And, thankfully the tests came out fine, and I'm healthy and well.

Let me know what you guys think of this. I've never really tried measuring before and having to do it for medical reasons was an interesting experience.
