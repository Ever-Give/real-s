I’ve mentioned before on here that I have chronic and severe mental illness and that it runs in my family. A university in my city was looking for people with a family history of mental illness to participate in a research study. It was a paid research study and seemed like an easy and interesting way to make some extra cash. I signed up and was accepted.

The research lab was in the basement of the university hospital. The “lab” was really just a suite of rooms with cubicles set up inside. It wasn’t cold, clinical or uncomfortable at all.

They had to hook me up to an EEG machine. So they had to take this cap that looked kind of like a swimming cap and put it over my head. The cap had a bunch of little nodes all over it. Then they had to take a bunch of wires and plug each wire into a node on the cap. This allowed them to record my brain waves. I had to sit very still while they were attaching all of the wires and then testing each wire to make sure it was properly connected. If any wire wasn’t properly picking up my brain waves, then they had to adjust it.

After I was all hooked up, I had to go into a small sound-proofed booth, and the researcher would talk to me through a little microphone. In the booth was a computer that I had to use to play computer games.

Here’s the part that becomes some sort of weird omorashi fantasy. In order for the EEG wires to keep picking up my brain waves, I had to sit completely still. I also had to concentrate on keeping my face in a neutral, relaxed position. While playing the computer games, I couldn’t furrow my eyebrows in concentration or smile when I won. In fact, if I moved my face too much, it messed up the EEG readings, and the researcher would remind me to go back to a neutral facial expression. I also had to really focus on the games, because they weren’t easy. It was stuff like being shown a series of pictures and having to recall them perfectly. I really wanted to do well on the games, because by doing well on the games you could win extra money.

It was really dark and cozy in the sound-proof booth, so of course I started to get sleepy. The researcher had warned me that this had happened to other participants, and in order to help me stay awake…they offered me drinks. Yep, you can see where this is going. This is a situation where you have to stay perfectly still, have to concentrate on a mentally-demanding task, and people are encouraging you to drink as much as you want in order to stay awake. If you were to ask for a bathroom break it would be a huge inconvenience to the researchers, because they would have to unhook all of your EEG wires, then hook them all back up and check them again, so you are literally trapped and can’t possibly go to the bathroom for the duration of the study. I would say that the computer games lasted for about an hour then you have to sit there and let the researchers unhook you.

Both are things that go straight through me, so I definitely had to go by the end of the study, but, like I said, it was nowhere near a desperate situation. I was still able to keep my body and my facial expression perfectly still enough for the EEG to pick up my brain waves and to be able to concentrate well enough on the computer games to win some extra money.
