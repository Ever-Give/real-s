A memory from China

Author: Sunfyre

*000*2025-08-25 13:35

There haven't been many instances of desperation I've seen in my personal life (Which is why I mostly write fiction hehe), but there is one that I remember from a long time ago.

I used to visit China every summer because my extended family lives there. The public bathroom situation then wasn't so great, they were often hard to find and not very clearly marked.

On this particular day, my parents took me to the subway station. We stopped somewhere to buy tickets, and while I was waiting I was just randomly looking around. Suddenly, something caught my eye: a middle-aged woman with medium length brown hair, a blue cardigan, tight white pants, and a cardigan walked briskly into the subway station. She looked like she was in distress, and ran over to one of the station employees. Now even back then, I was already fascinated by people peeing their pants. I saw subtle signs: legs pressed together, slightly bent over, hands over her crotch. She must have been bursting to pee but was trying to make it not too obvious in public.

From that distance, I was able to just make out what they were saying in mandarin. The woman asked "please tell me you have a bathroom here". The station employee pointed somewhere and said "the bathroom is on the left down that way". The woman, looking relieved, said "oh thank god you guys have a bathroom!" and quickly rushed in their direction, out of my sight.

I only saw this interaction briefly, but it still sticks with me today, even after almost ten years have passed. So many questions pass through my mind, which I wish I could know the answers to. Where was she coming from, and why was she so desperate? How badly did she have to go? If there wasn't a bathroom in the station, would she have been able to make it somewhere else? In real life, often we can only see snapshots of desperation, and we have to fill in the rest with our own fantasies.

Has anyone else seen a stranger desperate on the subway? How did it end?
