I’ve been a long time lurker here, just signed out and scrolling through stories and stuff, so I decided I’d actually try and do something today! I’ve seen some pretty insane holding stories on here, but I didn’t wanna do anything crazy. Honestly this is probably gonna be very lame in comparison to most here, but here we go anyways!

So I started at around 8:10-20 ish, didn’t really keep track of time too well which isn’t the best on my part, so sorry! But either way, I got myself my 40 ounce water bottle and a separate water bottle that was maybe…25? I’m not entirely sure, because the big 40 oz water bottle is metal and says how big it is on the bottom, and the plastic smaller one does t have any real markings. I also got a white monster, just a normal size 16 oz can.

I was actually on a discord call with a friend when this happened, like when the impulse to do a hold hit I mean. So I went and filled up my water bottles, then set a timer for 1 hour to drink both water bottles. I guessed it would be a little over 2 liters, maybe? Because 2 liters would be 70 ounces roughly, so 40 oz plus the other water bottle plus the white monster would definitely be over two liters.

ANYWAYS, I got both water bottles filled and started the 1 hour timer. Managed to get the 40 oz down within an hour easily, but I know the timer ran out at some point but I didn’t noticed because it was done by the time I checked in again, and I didn’t hear it. I’d say I got both water bottles in me at around 1 hour 10-20 minutes? But only because I lost track of time, if I wasn’t focused on the call with my friend and actually paid attention to the timer, I’d probably have gotten all 3 drinks in me easily enough.

Anyways, it started to build kinda slow at first then really quickly ramped up. By the time I was maybe 2/3’s of the way into my plastic bottle I was at a solid 6-7 out of ten. And when I finished it, I was at least at an 8. I ended up not finishing my white monster, because my stomach hit me with that awful gluggy feeling when you drink too much water and that makes me feel VERY nauseous. But either way, I distracted myself with the call and with playing The Pathless, which is BY FAR my favorite game of all time.

Anyways, 1 and a half-2 hours in, I could no longer hold it. My friend and I actually have a running joke where we both bring the mic with us to go when we piss, it’s always funny because I’ll randomly hear my friend start pissing out of nowhere since he’s quiet enough when he moves that I don’t notice he’s left his pc. At least for me, it’s easier because I’m on mobile since I can’t afford a pc in this economy.
