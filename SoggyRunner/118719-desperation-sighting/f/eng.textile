Desperation sighting

*000*2025-12-07 19:17

I witnessed some obvious pee desperation last week at work. Our building is right on the sidewalk, and the main entryway has glass storefront with a glass door, providing a clear view of the sidewalk outside. My office is on the second floor, off of an open landing just above the main entryway, and I have a clear view of the front door and sidewalk from my desk. Mid afternoon last Friday, a woman parked in front of our building and was standing on the sidewalk by her car typing on her phone.

I normally don’t pay much attention as there is a large apartment complex across the street from us and people going there are constantly parking in front of our building, but she caught my attention because she was stepping from foot to foot in what appeared to be a subtle pee dance as she typed. She was attractive, mid-thirties with shoulder length brown hair, and wearing black slacks with a white blouse. She finished typing, and stood looking around while continuing to bounce and step in what was now an obvious pee dance. After no more than a minute, she looked at her phone again, then walked up to our front door and rang the bell.

Our receptionist was out for the day, so I started to stand up to answer the door, when my colleague called out from his office, “It’s for me. I’ll get it.”

At this point, I was standing near my office door with a clear view of her waiting outside. It seemed she couldn’t stand still, and was constantly swaying and stepping side to side as she waited. She didn’t appear to be trying to hide her need, and I assume she thought no one could see. At one point, she even did a little crouch with her knees together for a couple of seconds. Seeing how obviously desperate she was, I was about to answer the door so as to not keep her waiting when my colleague appeared.

He invited her in, and they stood in the entryway briefly shaking hands and talking. I found out later she was a sales rep he had never met in person before, so introductions were in order. She continued swaying from foot to foot as they talked for a minute or so before heading up the stairs toward our offices. Just as they passed my door, I heard her ask if he minded if she used the restroom before they continued talking, and she excused herself before hurrying down the hall and disappearing into the women's. It was pretty tame, but I rarely see such obvious desperation in public and it was a nice treat to witness on a slow afternoon.
