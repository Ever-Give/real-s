June 2022. I was an 18 year old at the time. For quite some time I had been looking forward to the summer holidays along with one of my friends I had known since the first day in secondary school. Best friends you might say, for there was nobody else comparable to her out of all the other people I knew - Family don't count. For months we had worked our backsides off to try and claw into the higher reaches of the marking for our classes. As with any student, the agony was not just sitting the exam and hoping all is well but the waiting game afterwards, desperately praying for good fortune.

Sitting around and worrying was not my style at this moment in time. I did have a part-time job, but it would not keep me occupied. This was where I was rather surprised by my friend's offer to come stay with her and her family for a sleepover for several days. About 60 miles away from where we lived there was a caravan park. I had held sleepovers in the past, but only 1 of them had been with my friend and it was shared with other people. This would be hanging out with my best buddy having nothing but fun for several days straight.

Preparations were made and it was agreed that I would spend almost a week with my friend, starting on the Monday and getting home on the Saturday evening. I was ecstatic at this, even more so when I was invited over to her home the evening before for binging a TV show and having a small party between us. Arriving on the Sunday evening I was lounging about in my pyjamas and ready for the fun. Sweets, drinks and many hours later we decided to call it quits. The fun would commence at the caravan park.

As the morning rolled onwards I was treated to a new food for the first time - Pizza. Yes. At 18 years old I had never eaten a pizza. I am quite the picky eater and absolutely loved curries for any takeaway meal... Unfortunately for my past self, this pizza would lead on to experiencing a rather unfortunate realization. Taking my first bite my mouth was filled with flavour. This food tasted amazing, so I continued and without the assistance of my friend I had scoffed the full thing. She had her own of course, her parents sharing a third between themselves. (Apparently it is viewed as a 'dirty' thing to eat cold pizza in the morning? I dunno.)

I don't know why its so significant, but it was in that moment where I was trying to help my friend for the first time that I had come to the realization that there doesn't have to be multiple reasons for friendship. Gone was the thought of there needing to be positives and negatives. My friend was my friend because I liked her company and she liked mine, regardless of what happened.
