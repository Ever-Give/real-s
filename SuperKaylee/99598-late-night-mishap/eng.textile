Late Night `Mishap`

Author: SuperKaylee

*000*2024-04-01 11:19

It's been a while since I last made a post. Exams have been coming up and between the stresses of essay assignments and trying to maintain a social life I've found myself slipping. Definitely doesn't help that on the social side of things I'm also battling a magical illness commonly referred to as a 'crush'. Granted, I'm in the younger age bracket as it is but between the nervous giddy behaviour, my lacking charisma skills and the very recent onset of accidents... Well, I'm sure you get the overall picture of how chaotic life feels right now 🫠

In order to try and find equilibrium in my life I have taken up doing regular walks. I've never been one for lifting weights in a gym and whilst I love riding a bike, I prefer one that physically moves. On the plus side, the scenery around where I am is beautiful. I've always enjoyed walks through forests and the like. They provide a sense of peace and comfort that is hard to find anywhere else, like a tranquility that I can only presume druids must relate to.

Although my recent my walks to clear my head have been working wonders for my head, they have also rapidly changed from when I started doing them. First it would be only an hour during the afternoon, then I'd come crash back in my dorm and browse around here for awhile. Steadily, I started going both later and longer, returning when it was becoming quite dark. "Unfortunately" this seems to have also drawn out the perverse side of Kaylee that has been awakening since introducing herself to the community. The last few weeks have gone from enjoying the walks and the serenity of the wild to, well, still doing that, yet also pushing the limits of her holding capabilities. At the start of March I would occasionally find myself hurrying back with growing pains and urges, happy and relieved to not have a spot of bother by the time I was welcomed by my en-suite.

Now? Well... 🫠

The latest occurred last weekend, mostly because I've been away between then and now. Following an awful day of lectures and the looming pressures of my assignments this urge to flee my home and run away took flight. Leaving the confines of my dorm I jogged under the setting sun. Usually I make sure I'm feeling empty before I go, but I didn't this time. I'm no superb athlete, but I'd like to think I'm in reasonable shape, despite the few pounds I've put on recently. Usually I tire after about twenty minutes of a steady pace, but with the added distractions of the occasional bladder pang I slowed down earlier. The bottle of water I had downed during this jog definitely worked against me, the sloshing liquids making a discomfort arise.

Multitasking a belly massage and walking on ahead helped settle my nerves and bodily concerns for awhile until I reached the destination I had in mind. Through the forest I normally exercise through there's a quarry. Following the path upwards on its outskirts leads curious souls like me to a beautiful scene of a small waterfall and a high ground view of the fields nearby. Part of it is obscured by trees, but that in itself is part of the charm in my eyes

Normally I hang around here for a while and meditate. Trying to do that this time around was difficult, my body nagging at me to stop playing games and behave. As if possessed by a demonic influence I instead ignored this with a chuckle, throwing my concerns into the wind as I just sat and admired the view.

Usually I would head back, the desperation then becoming apparent. This was the first time I was so desperate no where near a private abode. Those of you who know me know I detest public bathrooms due to their cleanliness and lack of privacy. Out here, I was entirely alone with the world.

The sheltered part of my mind urged me to go against my principles and, just this once at least, drop my pride. Nobody would see me, yet the voice was instead drowned out by this general naughty guilt.

"What if...?"

I remember standing by the edge, looking at the waterfall and just letting all my worries go. Twin rivers soon spread down my jeans, soaking every bit of fabric they could reach. In my blind lust for indulging in this I neglected to take my shoes off... What was usually a "yuck!" Instead was a "haha, *oops*"

The warmth clung to me for a while, fading as it usually does. The time spent reaching here and then enjoying the scenery saw the sunset turn towards darkness. My moment of recklessness had now punished me with a colder walk back. The guilt came back stronger and despite no witnesses being in sight, I wrapped my jacket around my waist and set back with a gulp and probably a sheepish look to myself.

The walk back took a lot longer. My moment of fun had turned to disappointment as I neared the dorm, hastily rushing into my room without being seen. With a locked door secured I stepped into my en-suite, stood in the shower and decided to just let go of anything else that might be there. Several drops at most.

I picture the experience like scenes in cartoons with a miniature angel and devil on my shoulders, the angel scolding me while the devil pats me on the back.

...I know it's bad, but it just feels so enjoyable. I know I shouldn't go publicly, so I instead go far away from prying eyes where I won't cause any upset or further shame.

I write this feeling both happy and bad that it's spiralled so much, but then I realise how much it helps my peace of mind and settles me... And then I look outside my window and wonder where the next time will be.
