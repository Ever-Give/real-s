Friend Peed her bed

Author: stubger

*000*2020-12-16 03:12

This story was told to me by one of my best friends. She laughs about it to this day. Her name has been changed for the story.

Several years ago my friend, we'll call her Lauren, was in college. She is a dark haired burnet. Tall for a woman, 5' 9" and fit. Lauren had a break for Christmas and went to go see her mom. However, this year was different. Her mom wanted to travel. They decided to meet up in Portland, Oregon. The first day they just checked into their hotel. It was late at night around 1 am and Lauren was tired, so they went to sleep. She and mom shared a room with two queen sized beds. They both woke up early so they could explore the town. They went all over town on foot, sampling the popular donut shop, going to the rose gardens, looking through art museums, etc. After hours of walking, exploring, and sampling the food of Portland they headed back to the hotel around midnight. Lauren called up a friend and talked so her mom decided she would take a shower.

At this point Lauren had not stopped for a bathroom break for half the day. She didn't realize she had to go that bad until her mom was in the shower and she had hung up with her friend from college. Laying on the bed she thought she would simply wait for mom to get out. However, two late nights in a row took their toll. She fell asleep watching tv in bed waiting for the bathroom. When her mom came out, she covered her daughter, still fully dressed in jeans and a t-shirt, with the blankets and turned out the lights. According to Lauren she must have slept pretty hard, because when she woke up she slowly realized she had pissed the bed. Not just a little bit either, she "completely soaked her jeans up to her shirt." Of course, she had to get up out of bed, and her mom immediately saw that her beautiful 18 year old daughter had just wet her bed.

I don't know what happened after that. Lauren has only told three of her good friends about this incident. She was quite embarrassed. However, when telling the story, I thought I heard a little bit of intrigue in her voice as she described waking up to a puddle in a hotel room bed. Unfortunately, I have not had the opportunity to explore this side of Lauren further. Sometimes, I slip in a joke about her needing goodnights, but that's about the extent of it.
